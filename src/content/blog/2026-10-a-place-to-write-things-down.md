Most of what I make ends up in a private repository, a chat log with an agent, or a
note in my vault that nobody else will ever open. That is fine for the work. It is less
fine for the part I actually enjoy, which is the moment something finally clicks.

So this is a log. Not tutorials, not hot takes. Small things I built, broke, fixed or
learned, written down roughly when they happen:

- side projects, and the agents that help me build them
- hardware that got a second life on my desk
- the occasional bike ride or Lego set that turned into an engineering problem

Some entries will be long. Most will be short. If one of them saves you an evening, it
was worth writing.
